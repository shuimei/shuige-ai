Common questions

How is pricing structured?

A fixed price for the whole project, split across milestones. Small, well-defined jobs such as a scraper can be quoted outright. If the requirements are still exploratory, we start with a short paid spike and decide on the rest afterwards.

How long does it take?

Data collection is usually 1–2 weeks. Knowledge bases and AI integrations are usually 3–6 weeks. Model training depends on data volume and labelling cost. You get a concrete schedule once we have talked through the scope.

I only have a vague idea — is that enough to start?

Yes, and that is how most projects begin. Half an hour is usually enough to turn it into a scope that can be built and accepted, and then we decide whether to go ahead.

Can you take over a half-finished project?

Yes, but I read the code first and give you an assessment: what can be kept, what I would rewrite, and where the risks are. You decide what happens next after reading it.

How is data kept secure?

Your data can stay on your own servers or inside your network — I work from anonymised samples. I am happy to sign an NDA, and where data residency rules apply, the model can be deployed on your own hardware.

What happens after delivery?

You get the source, deployment notes and design docs, plus a walkthrough. I stay available for questions for an agreed period written into the contract — I do not disappear the day it ships.
